Privacy policy

Short version: I don't collect your personal info. No analytics, no ads, no accounts, no cookies.

Last updated September 25, 2026

What this site stores

Nothing on my end. Two small things are saved in your own browser so the site remembers them next time:

  • Your theme (light or dark), if you use the toggle.
  • Your boop count, if you boop.

These never leave your device and I can't see them. Clearing your browser's site data removes them. See the cookie policy for details.

Services your browser talks to

The site is plain HTML, but a few parts load from other services. When they do, those services receive the usual technical info any website gets, like your IP address and browser type.

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and delivers every page. It processes requests to keep the site fast and protected. Cloudflare's privacy policy
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts. Google Fonts privacy FAQ
  • Lanyard (api.lanyard.rest) provides my Discord status in the menu bar. Your browser asks it for my status; nothing about you is sent beyond the request itself. About Lanyard
  • YouTube provides the video thumbnails on the portfolio page. Videos only load when you click one, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Once a video plays, Google's privacy policy applies.

If you contact me

If you email me or message me on Discord, I'll see whatever you send, like your name, email address, or username. I only use it to talk with you. I don't sell it, share it, or add you to any mailing list.

Links to other sites

Links to TikTok, YouTube, GitHub, Discord and other sites take you somewhere with its own privacy policy. I'm not responsible for what they do.

Changes

If this changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
