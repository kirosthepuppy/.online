Cookie policy

This site doesn't set any cookies. Not even the snack kind. 🍪

Last updated September 25, 2026

Cookies

kirothepup.online doesn't set cookies, and I don't use tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies.

Local storage

Instead of cookies, the site saves two small preferences in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are never sent to me or anyone else.

NameWhat it doesHow long
themeRemembers light or dark mode, if you pick oneUntil you clear it
boopsRemembers how many times you booped meUntil you clear it

To remove them, clear this site's data in your browser settings. The site still works fine without them.

Other services

  • YouTube: the videos on the portfolio page use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and don't load until you click one. Once a video plays, YouTube may store cookies or similar data on your device under Google's cookie policy.
  • Cloudflare: the site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may set a security cookie if it needs to check for bots or attacks. Cloudflare's cookie policy

For everything else about your data, see the privacy policy.
